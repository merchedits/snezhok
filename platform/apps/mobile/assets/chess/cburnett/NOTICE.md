## Cburnett chess pieces

The chess-piece artwork in this directory is by Colin M. L. Burnett. Snezhok
obtained the SVG source from the Lichess `cburnett` mirror at revision
`5f25d6d433ae95ce1f6a92baababb14f98658596` and rasterized it to transparent
180 by 180 PNG files without changing the design.

Source: https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/tree/5f25d6d433ae95ce1f6a92baababb14f98658596/public/piece/cburnett
